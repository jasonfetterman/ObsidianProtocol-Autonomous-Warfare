Sunburn (Desert / Canyon / Long‑Range Fire & Air Superiority)

Executive Summary

Sunburn is a blistering desert‑canyon battlefield built for long‑range firepower, air superiority, high‑altitude reconnaissance, and autonomous maneuvering across open and vertical terrain. The environment features sandstone canyons, wind‑carved ridges, dry riverbeds, elevated plateaus, abandoned mining sites, and sparse desert settlements. The map emphasizes Obsidian Protocol’s autonomous long ‑range targeting logic, air‑ground coordination, sensor‑fusion under heat distortion, and logistics under extreme desert conditions. In AR (Nreal Light/Air), the map anchors at ~4–5 m viewing distance, covering a 10×10 m physical area representing ~1 km² of canyon ‑desert terrain. Key features include canyon sightlines, high‑ground air corridors, long‑range artillery zones, and heat‑driven sensor degradation.

Design Goals & Gameplay

Long‑Range Desert Firepower
Sunburn prioritizes snipers, artillery, missile vehicles, and long‑range drones. Open desert flats and canyon ridges create extreme sightlines.

Air Superiority & High‑Altitude Recon

Aircraft, VTOLs, and drones dominate the vertical dimension. Air corridors between canyon walls define recon, interception, and strike patterns.

Canyon Maneuvering & Vertical Combat
Ground units navigate narrow canyon passes, elevated ridges, and dry riverbeds. Verticality creates ambush zones and long‑range overwatch positions.

Autonomy & Heat‑Aware Pathfinding

Autonomous units choose routes based on heat shimmer, elevation, cover scarcity, and sensor clarity. Units avoid exposed flats during peak heat unless necessary.

Sensor & Information Warfare

Heat distortion, dust storms, mirage effects, and thermal plumes degrade sensors. Relay towers on ridges restore clarity. Destroying relays creates blind pockets across the canyon network.

Logistics & Resources
Fuel depots, mining stations, and desert outposts serve as resupply nodes. Convoys must navigate exposed roads and avoid ambush ‑prone canyon passes.

Deployment & Progress
A fixed deployment budget (~10,000 points) forces players to choose between long‑range artillery, air dominance, or canyon‑adapted armor. Blue deploys from the southwest dry‑river staging zone; Red deploys from the northeast ridge‑top command.

Spatial Constraints (Nreal AR)

Device Capabilities
Nreal Light/Air: ~53° diagonal FOV, 1920×1080 per‑eye resolution. Optimal viewing distance ~4–5 m.

Playable Area & Scaling
10×10 m physical area ≈ 1 km² canyon‑desert zone (1:100 scale). Designed for tabletop AR.

Tracking & Occlusion
Inside‑out tracking detects horizontal planes. Depth‑mesh (if available) provides limited occlusion. Virtual objects render over real‑world view; assume minimal real‑world occlusion.
