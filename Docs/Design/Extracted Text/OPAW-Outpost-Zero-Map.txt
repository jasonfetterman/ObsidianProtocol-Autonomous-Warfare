Outpost Zero (Remote Arctic / Extreme Environment, Limited Resources)

Executive Summary

Outpost Zero is a harsh Arctic battlefield built for extreme‑environment warfare, limited‑resource logistics, survival‑driven autonomy, and sensor‑degraded combat. The terrain features frozen tundra, ice ridges, research domes, buried bunkers, geothermal vents, supply caches, and wind‑blasted plateaus. The map emphasizes Obsidian Protocol’s autonomous cold‑weather logic, resource‑rationing behaviors, sensor‑fusion under blizzards, and logistics under severe environmental constraints. In AR (Nreal Light/Air), the map anchors at ~4–5 m viewing distance, covering a 10×10 m physical area representing ~1 km² of Arctic terrain. Key features include whiteout zones, limited‑resource supply nodes, frozen chokepoints, and survival‑driven tactical decision‑making.

Design Goals & Gameplay

Extreme‑Environment Warfare
Outpost Zero prioritizes cold‑weather hazards: freezing temperatures, ice storms, low visibility, and equipment degradation. Units must adapt to environmental threats as much as enemy fire.
Limited Resources & Survival Logistics
Fuel, ammunition, and power are scarce. Controlling geothermal vents, supply caches, and research domes determines long‑term survivability.
Autonomy & Survival Pathfinding

Autonomous units choose routes based on wind exposure, ice stability, thermal cover, and resource availability. Units avoid whiteout zones unless necessary.
Sensor & Information Warfare

Blizzards, ice fog, thermal distortion, and magnetic interference degrade sensors. Relay towers on ridges restore clarity. Destroying relays creates massive blind pockets across the tundra.
Logistics & Resources
Fuel caches, geothermal stations, and research domes serve as resupply nodes. Convoys must navigate frozen terrain and avoid ambush‑prone ice corridors.
Deployment & Progress
A fixed deployment budget (~10,000 points) forces players to choose between survival‑optimized infantry, cold‑weather drones, or heavy Arctic armor. Blue deploys from the southwest landing zone; Red deploys from the northeast geothermal command ridge.

Spatial Constraints (Nreal AR)

Device Capabilities
Nreal Light/Air: ~53° diagonal FOV, 1920×1080 per‑eye resolution. Optimal viewing distance ~4–5 m.
Playable Area & Scaling

10×10 m physical area ≈ 1 km² Arctic zone (1:100 scale). Designed for tabletop AR.
Tracking & Occlusion
Inside‑out tracking detects horizontal planes. Depth‑mesh (if available) provides limited occlusion. Virtual objects render over real‑world view; assume minimal real‑world occlusion.

Frozen Trail Route

Predictable but safe; vulnerable to overwatch from ridges.
