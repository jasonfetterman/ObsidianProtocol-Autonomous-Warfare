TOTAL PEOPLE

For the movie I would plan around:

Core production team

~80–120 people

Full physical production

~150–250 people

Including VFX/post-production

~200–350+ people

You don't pay all 300 people for the same amount of time. Departments come in and out throughout development, pre-production, filming, VFX and post.

ROUGH MOVIE LABOR BUDGET

For a serious independent production:

Production level                       Approx. total production budget

Very low budget                        $5M–$10M

Low/mid budget                         $15M–$30M

Strong indie                           $30M–$60M

Studio-quality SF                      $60M–$100M+

Large studio                           $100M–$200M+

For this particular movie, I would personally target approximately $40M–$75M if the goal is to make the world, machines and battles look genuinely convincing without trying to compete with a $200M Hollywood blockbuster.

The biggest money should go toward VFX, production design, action, cinematography, sound, and the lead cast—not filling the production with unnecessary personnel.

And because the story is specifically about autonomous warfare, you can make the armies enormous on screen without needing thousands of human performers. The audience sees thousands of machines, while the physical production may only need a relatively small number of real actors and practical machines.
