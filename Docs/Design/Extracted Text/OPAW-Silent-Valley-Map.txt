Silent Valley (Rural / Mountain / Recon & Stealth)

Executive Summary

Silent Valley is a rural‑mountain reconnaissance battlefield built for stealth operations, autonomous search, low‑visibility movement, and precision recon across layered elevation. The terrain features forested slopes, narrow mountain passes, abandoned farmsteads, rocky outcrops, mist‑filled valleys, cliffside trails, and hidden observation posts. The map emphasizes Obsidian Protocol’s autonomous recon logic, sensor‑fusion under mountain fog and foliage occlusion, comms‑relay warfare, and logistics across steep, uneven terrain. In AR (Nreal Light/Air), the map anchors at ~4–5 m viewing distance, covering a 10×10 m physical area representing ~1 km² of rural ‑mountain terrain. Key features include stealth corridors, elevation‑driven sightlines, concealed movement routes, and recon‑focused engagements.

Design Goals & Gameplay

Stealth‑Focused Mountain Recon
Silent Valley prioritizes scouting, tracking, and infiltration. Forest cover, fog pockets, and elevation shifts create natural blind zones.
Autonomous Search & Terrain‑Aware Pathfinding
Units navigate steep slopes, narrow passes, rocky ledges, and forested trails. Autonomous logic selects routes based on concealment, elevation advantage, and sensor clarity.

Concealment & Ambush Warfare
Trees, rocks, mist, and rural structures create stealth pockets. Ambushes, flanking, and sudden close‑range engagements dominate.

Sensor & Information Warfare
Fog, humidity, vegetation clutter, thermal distortion, and mountain occlusion degrade sensors. Relay towers on ridges restore clarity. Destroying relays creates blind pockets across the valley.

Logistics & Resources
Fuel caches, farmsteads, and mountain cabins serve as resupply nodes. Convoys must navigate narrow roads and avoid ambush‑prone forest corridors.

Deployment & Progress
A fixed deployment budget (~10,000 points) forces players to choose between stealth infantry, recon drones, or mountain‑adapted armor. Blue deploys from the southwest rural farmstead; Red deploys from the northeast ridge‑top command cabin.

Spatial Constraints (Nreal AR)

Device Capabilities
Nreal Light/Air: ~53° diagonal FOV, 1920×1080 per‑eye resolution. Optimal viewing distance ~4–5 m.

Playable Area & Scaling
10×10 m physical area ≈ 1 km² rural‑mountain zone (1:100 scale). Designed for tabletop AR.

Tracking & Occlusion
Inside‑out tracking detects horizontal planes. Depth‑mesh (if available) provides limited occlusion. Virtual objects render over real‑world view; assume minimal real‑world occlusion.

Chokepoints & Sightlines

Stealth Corridor
Severe visibility loss; ideal for stealth ambushes and sensor deception.

Ridge Network
Interior flanking routes; unpredictable cover due to elevation and foliage.

Valley Basin
Long‑range engagements broken by vegetation; fog reduces accuracy.

Mountain Trail Route
Predictable but safe; vulnerable to ridge overwatch.

Sightlines
Ridge Relay A provides long‑range sensor coverage (~300 m). Fog and canopy shadows create fog‑of‑war pockets.

Deployment Zones

Blue Deployment: SW farmstead + recon staging.
Red Deployment: NE ridge‑top command cabin + thermal defenses.
Neutral Forward Zone: Outpost at (500,950) for drone drops.
