The Divide (Mountain Pass / Strategic Chokepoint Warfare)

Executive Summary

The Divide is a high‑altitude mountain‑pass battlefield built for strategic chokepoint warfare, elevation‑driven combat, autonomous route control, and multi‑domain coordination across narrow, high‑risk terrain. The environment features sheer cliffs, winding passes, avalanche‑scarred slopes, fortified ridgelines, collapsed tunnels, old military outposts, and steep switchback roads. The map emphasizes Obsidian Protocol’s autonomous elevation ‑aware routing, sensor‑fusion under mountain fog and rock occlusion, comms‑relay warfare, and logistics under severe terrain constraints. In AR (Nreal Light/Air), the map anchors at ~4–5 m viewing distance, covering a 10×10 m physical area representing ~1 km² of mountain ‑pass terrain. Key features include chokepoint control, overwatch ridges, ambush corners, and high‑risk mobility corridors.

Design Goals & Gameplay

Chokepoint‑Focused Warfare

The Divide prioritizes control of narrow passes, switchbacks, and cliffside trails. Holding key chokepoints determines reinforcement flow and strategic dominance.

Elevation‑Driven Combat
High‑ground ridges provide overwatch for snipers, artillery, and drones. Verticality shapes sightlines, movement, and engagement ranges.

Ambush & Route Denial

Blind corners, collapsed tunnels, and narrow ledges create ideal ambush zones. Route denial becomes a core strategic tool.

Autonomy & Terrain‑Aware Routing

Autonomous units choose routes based on elevation advantage, rockfall hazards, fog density, and sensor clarity. Units adapt to blocked roads, unstable slopes, and shifting mountain conditions.

Sensor & Information Warfare

Fog, dust, rock occlusion, thermal plumes, and elevation clutter degrade sensors. Relay towers on ridges restore clarity. Destroying relays creates blind pockets across the pass.

Logistics & Resources
Fuel caches, mountain cabins, and old military outposts serve as resupply nodes. Convoys must navigate narrow roads and avoid ambush ‑prone switchbacks.

Deployment & Progress
A fixed deployment budget (~10,000 points) forces players to choose between fast armor, overwatch specialists, or stealth mountain infantry. Blue deploys from the southwest lower ‑pass gate; Red deploys from the northeast ridge‑top fortress.

Spatial Constraints (Nreal AR)

Device Capabilities
Nreal Light/Air: ~53° diagonal FOV, 1920×1080 per‑eye resolution. Optimal viewing distance ~4–5 m.

Playable Area & Scaling
10×10 m physical area ≈ 1 km² mountain‑pass zone (1:100 scale). Designed for tabletop AR.

Tracking & Occlusion
Inside‑out tracking detects horizontal planes. Depth‑mesh (if available) provides limited occlusion. Virtual objects render over real‑world view; assume minimal real‑world occlusion.
