Mirage (Desert / Military / Deception & Information Warfare)

Executive Summary

Mirage is a military desert battlefield built for deception, misinformation, sensor manipulation, and autonomous counter‑recon operations. The terrain features heat‑warped flats, dune fields, military outposts, radar stations, decoy emplacements, false infrastructure, buried cables, and canyon‑edge observation posts. The map emphasizes Obsidian Protocol’s autonomous deception logic, sensor‑fusion under mirage distortion, comms‑relay spoofing, and logistics under extreme heat and visibility anomalies. In AR (Nreal Light/Air), the map anchors at ~4–5 m viewing distance, covering a 10×10 m physical area representing ~1 km² of desert‑military terrain. Key features include decoy zones, false sensor signatures, long‑range mirage corridors, and information‑warfare‑driven engagements.

Design Goals & Gameplay

Deception‑Driven Warfare

Mirage prioritizes false signals, decoy units, spoofed radar returns, holographic projections, and misinformation traps. Players manipulate enemy perception more than terrain.
Information Warfare & Sensor Manipulation

Heat shimmer, mirage distortion, dust haze, and thermal plumes degrade sensors. Specialized units generate false signatures, jam comms, or create phantom targets.
Autonomy & Counter‑Recon Logic

Autonomous units choose routes based on sensor clarity, deception zones, false positives, and enemy spoofing attempts. Units adapt to misleading data and prioritize verification.
Desert Military Operations
Long‑range fire, drone reconnaissance, armored pushes, and radar‑guided strikes dominate. Outposts and radar stations serve as both real and decoy objectives.
Logistics & Resources
Fuel depots, comms bunkers, and desert roads serve as resupply nodes. Convoys must navigate exposed flats and avoid ambush‑prone deception corridors.
Deployment & Progress
A fixed deployment budget (~10,000 points) forces players to choose between deception units, long‑range firepower, or sensor‑resistant armor. Blue deploys from the southwest dune‑base staging area; Red deploys from the northeast radar‑spire command.

Spatial Constraints (Nreal AR)

Device Capabilities
Nreal Light/Air: ~53° diagonal FOV, 1920×1080 per‑eye resolution. Optimal viewing distance ~4–5 m.
Playable Area & Scaling
10×10 m physical area ≈ 1 km² desert‑military zone (1:100 scale). Designed for tabletop AR.
Tracking & Occlusion
Inside‑out tracking detects horizontal planes. Depth‑mesh (if available) provides limited occlusion. Virtual objects render over real‑world view; assume minimal real‑world occlusion.
