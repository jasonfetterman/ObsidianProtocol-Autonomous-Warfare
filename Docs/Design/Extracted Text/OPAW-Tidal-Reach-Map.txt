Tidal Reach (Island / Ocean / Naval Warfare & Amphibious Operations)

Executive Summary

Tidal Reach is a tropical island–ocean hybrid battlefield built for naval warfare, amphibious landings, littoral combat, and autonomous multi‑domain coordination. The terrain features coral shallows, deep‑water channels, sandy beaches, mangrove swamps, cliffside fortifications, island villages, and offshore naval platforms. The map emphasizes Obsidian Protocol’s autonomous amphibious routing, naval‑air coordination, sensor‑fusion across water and land, and logistics under dynamic tide conditions. In AR (Nreal Light/Air), the map anchors at ~4–5 m viewing distance, covering a 10×10 m physical area representing ~1 km² of island‑ocean terrain. Key features include landing beaches, naval chokepoints, airspace corridors, reef hazards, and multi‑domain combat across sea, air, and land.

Design Goals & Gameplay

Naval Warfare in Littoral Waters
Tidal Reach prioritizes ship‑to‑ship combat, torpedo lanes, reef navigation, and hull‑hazard maneuvering. Coral shelves create dynamic cover and movement obstacles.
Amphibious Operations & Beach Landings

Players conduct coordinated landings using amphibious armor, drones, and infantry. Beaches, mangroves, and cliffs create layered defensive zones.
Air Operations & Reconnaissance
VTOL aircraft, drones, and long‑range recon planes operate above the island chain. Airspace corridors between cliffs and offshore platforms define movement and targeting.
Autonomy & Multi‑Domain Coordination

Autonomous units coordinate naval, air, and land forces simultaneously. Units adapt to shifting tides, fog pockets, and sonar interference.
Sensor & Information Warfare

Sea spray, humidity, thermal distortion, sonar scatter, and magnetic interference degrade sensors. Relay towers on cliffs restore clarity. Destroying relays creates blind pockets across the island chain.
Logistics & Resources
Fuel caches, island depots, and offshore platforms serve as resupply nodes. Convoys must navigate beach approaches and avoid ambush‑prone mangrove corridors.
Deployment & Progress

A fixed deployment budget (~10,000 points) forces players to choose between naval dominance, air superiority, or amphibious mobility. Blue deploys from the southwest offshore staging platform; Red deploys from the northeast cliffside fortress.

Spatial Constraints (Nreal AR)

Device Capabilities
Nreal Light/Air: ~53° diagonal FOV, 1920×1080 per‑eye resolution. Optimal viewing distance ~4–5 m.
Playable Area & Scaling
10×10 m physical area ≈ 1 km² island‑ocean zone (1:100 scale). Designed for tabletop AR.
Tracking & Occlusion
Inside‑out tracking detects horizontal planes. Depth‑mesh (if available) provides limited occlusion. Virtual objects render over real‑world view; assume minimal real‑world occlusion.
