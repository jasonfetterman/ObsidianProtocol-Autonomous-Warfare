Steel City (Major Urban / Large‑Scale Combined Arms)

Executive Summary

Steel City is a massive urban‑industrial megabattlefield built for large‑scale combined arms warfare, integrating armor, infantry, drones, artillery, air support, and autonomous units across dense city blocks and heavy industrial zones. The terrain features high ‑rise clusters, steelworks, rail corridors, elevated freeways, cargo terminals, fortified intersections, and multi ‑level combat spaces. The map emphasizes Obsidian Protocol’s autonomous coordination across multiple fronts, sensor‑network routing, comms‑relay warfare, and logistics under dense urban constraints. In AR (Nreal Light/Air), the map anchors at ~4–5 m viewing distance, covering a 10×10 m physical area representing ~1 km² of major urban terrain. Key features include multi ‑tier street networks, industrial choke zones, rooftop combat, and large‑scale combined arms engagements.

Design Goals & Gameplay

Large‑Scale Combined Arms Warfare

Steel City supports simultaneous armor pushes, infantry assaults, drone reconnaissance, artillery strikes, and air support. Multi ‑level terrain enables layered combat.
Urban‑Industrial Hybrid Combat

High‑rise districts provide vertical combat; industrial zones provide long‑range fire lanes; rail corridors enable flanking; elevated freeways create multi‑tier movement.
Autonomy & Multi‑Front Coordination

Autonomous units coordinate across multiple sectors, selecting optimal fronts to reinforce or breach based on sensor data and objective priority.

Sensor & Information Warfare

Smoke, dust, steelworks heat, and dense building layouts distort sensors. Relay towers on rooftops restore clarity. Destroying relays creates blind pockets across the city grid.

Logistics & Resources

Cargo terminals, steelworks, rail yards, and freeway checkpoints serve as resupply nodes. Convoys must navigate unpredictable street networks and avoid ambush ‑prone industrial corridors.

Deployment & Progress

A fixed deployment budget (~10,000 points) forces players to choose between heavy armor, air support, artillery, or autonomous infantry squads. Blue deploys from the southwest industrial yard; Red deploys from the northeast high ‑rise command tower.

Spatial Constraints (Nreal AR)

Device Capabilities

Nreal Light/Air: ~53° diagonal FOV, 1920×1080 per‑eye resolution. Optimal viewing distance ~4–5 m.

Playable Area & Scaling

10×10 m physical area ≈ 1 km² major urban zone (1:100 scale). Designed for tabletop AR.

Tracking & Occlusion

Inside‑out tracking detects horizontal planes. Depth‑mesh (if available) provides limited occlusion. Virtual objects render over real‑world view; assume minimal real‑world occlusion.

Layout Overview

Coordinate System

1000×1000 grid (1 unit ≈ 1 m). Origin (0,0) = southwest corner.
