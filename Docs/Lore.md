# ⚫ Obsidian Protocol — Lore & World Background

## The World
The year is unlisted by design. Obsidian Protocol does not anchor itself to a specific timeline — it anchors itself to a technological threshold. A moment where autonomous warfare became inevitable, and human command shifted from micromanagement to strategic intent.

Global conflict didn’t escalate because nations wanted war.  
It escalated because the battlefield evolved faster than humans could react.

## Black Steel Innovations
Black Steel Innovations began as a defense contractor specializing in autonomous reconnaissance systems. Their early work focused on drones, uplinks, and battlefield mapping. But as conflicts intensified, demand shifted from observation to action.

The Warden Program was the answer.

## The Warden Program
The Warden Program is a full-spectrum autonomous warfare initiative designed to replace traditional command structures with intelligent, self-directed units capable of:

- independent tactical decision-making  
- coordinated multi-unit operations  
- adaptive battlefield behavior  
- real-time threat analysis  
- autonomous siege and breach logic  

Every unit — air, ground, sea, command, experimental — is part of a single distributed intelligence network.

They don’t follow orders.  
They interpret intent.

## Autonomous Warfare
The battlefield is no longer a static map. It is a living environment shaped by:

- fog-of-war  
- destructible cover  
- elevation and terrain  
- suppression zones  
- visibility cones  
- dynamic hazards  

Squads adapt to these conditions without waiting for human input. They flank, breach, retreat, suppress, and coordinate based on their own tactical reasoning.

## The Conflict
Obsidian Protocol does not define a single enemy.  
The enemy is context — any force, faction, or threat that challenges the Warden Program’s objectives.

This allows the world to expand:

- rogue AI factions  
- corporate militaries  
- insurgent groups  
- synthetic entities  
- off-world expansions  

The Warden Program is the foundation.  
The world grows around it.

## The Vision
Obsidian Protocol is not just a game.  
It is a simulation of autonomous warfare — a testbed for emergent combat, intelligent systems, and modular faction design.

Every match tells a story.  
Every squad has intent.  
Every decision has weight.

This is the future of RTS.
