# DEVLOG #7 — Steam Page & Trailer Reveal

Welcome back to the development journey of **Obsidian Protocol — Autonomous Warfare**.

Today’s devlog marks a major milestone: the first public reveal of the project. This is where development crosses the threshold from internal iteration to public visibility — where players finally get to see what autonomous warfare looks like.

This devlog covers the **Reveal Trailer**, the **Steam Page**, and the **Public Launch Package**.

---

## 🎬 First Reveal Trailer — Cinematic Autonomous Warfare

The reveal trailer is designed to showcase the core identity of Obsidian Protocol:

- Autonomous squad intelligence  
- Intent‑based commanding  
- Siege & breach warfare  
- Living battlefields  
- Modular factions  
- High‑performance combat simulation  

The trailer focuses on **momentum**, **clarity**, and **cinematic impact** — showing squads thinking, reacting, and coordinating without micromanagement.

Key trailer beats include:

- A breach team detonating a wall  
- A support squad establishing a suppression line  
- A flanking team pushing through debris  
- Enemy AI coordinating a multi‑angle assault  
- Dynamic fog‑of‑war and destructible cover  
- Elevation‑based visibility and tactical overlays  

The goal is simple:  
Show players that RTS can evolve.
