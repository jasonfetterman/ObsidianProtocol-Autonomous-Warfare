# DEVLOG #3 — Siege, Breach, and Living Battlefields

Welcome back to the development journey of **Obsidian Protocol — Autonomous Warfare**.

Today’s devlog covers three major systems that define the identity of the game: **Siege AI**, **Breach Logic**, and **Living Battlefields**. These systems transform engagements from predictable skirmishes into dynamic, cinematic war stories.

---

## ⚔️ Siege AI — Assaults With Intent

Traditional RTS sieges are linear: units walk forward, shoot walls, and die.

Obsidian Protocol’s siege AI is different.

Enemy forces:

- Identify structural weak points  
- Establish firing lines  
- Suppress defenders  
- Deploy breach teams  
- Execute multi‑angle assaults  
- Adapt mid‑siege based on resistance  

Every siege feels like a coordinated military operation — not a scripted event.

---

## 💥 Breach Logic — Tactical Entry Systems

Breach teams evaluate:

- Wall thickness  
- Defender positions  
- Cover availability  
- Interior layout  
- Escape routes  
- Flanking opportunities  

Then they execute:

- Plant charge  
- Detonate  
- Push through  
- Clear interior  
- Hold breach point  

This creates dynamic, unpredictable engagements that feel alive.

---

## 🌑 Living Battlefields — War That Reacts

The battlefield itself is a character.

It reacts to everything:

- Fog‑of‑war  
- Destructible cover  
- Elevation advantages  
- Visibility cones  
- Suppression zones  
- Dynamic lighting  

Every fight feels different.  
Every decision matters.  
Every engagement tells a story.
