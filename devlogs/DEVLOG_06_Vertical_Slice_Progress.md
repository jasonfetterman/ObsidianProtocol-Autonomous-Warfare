# DEVLOG #6 — Vertical Slice Progress

Welcome back to the development journey of **Obsidian Protocol — Autonomous Warfare**.

Today’s devlog marks a major milestone: the **Vertical Slice**.  
This is the first fully playable snapshot of the game — a small, controlled environment where all core systems come together to prove the vision works in practice.

This devlog breaks down what’s currently implemented, what’s being refined, and what’s coming next.

---

## 🎮 Playable Skirmish Scenario

The vertical slice includes a compact battlefield designed to showcase:

- Autonomous squad behavior  
- Intent‑based commanding  
- Siege & breach encounters  
- Dynamic battlefield reactions  
- Prototype faction interactions  

It’s small, but it’s real gameplay — and it already feels alive.

---

## 🏗️ Prototype Structures

Early buildings are now functional:

- **Barracks** — trains infantry squads  
- **Supply Depot** — increases resource capacity  
- **Defense Turret** — autonomous perimeter defense  
- **Extractor** — pulls resources from the environment  
- **Breach Wall** — destructible structure for siege testing  

These structures establish the foundation for base building and battlefield control.

---

## ⚔️ Prototype Units

The vertical slice includes several early unit types:

- **Rifle Squad** — baseline autonomous infantry  
- **Breach Team** — specialized wall‑breaking unit  
- **Support Squad** — suppression and cover‑fire behavior  
- **Heavy Assault Unit** — slow, armored frontline pressure  
- **Recon Drone** — visibility, scouting, and elevation logic  

All units are fully autonomous and react to battlefield conditions.

---

## 🌑 Dynamic Battlefield Interactions

The slice demonstrates the core environmental systems:

- Fog‑of‑war  
- Destructible cover  
- Elevation advantages  
- Visibility cones  
- Suppression zones  
- Breachable structures  
- Tactical overlays  

These systems make every engagement feel reactive and cinematic.

---

## 🎨 Early VFX, SFX, and UI

The vertical slice includes first‑pass presentation elements:

- Muzzle flashes  
- Breach explosions  
- Suppression effects  
- Impact sparks  
- Tactical UI overlays  
- Early HUD mockups

These are placeholders, but they already enhance the feel of combat.

The slice is playable — now it’s about polish.
