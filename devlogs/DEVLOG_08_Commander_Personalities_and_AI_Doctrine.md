# DEVLOG #8 — Commander Personalities & AI Doctrine

Welcome back to the development journey of **Obsidian Protocol — Autonomous Warfare**.

Today’s devlog dives into one of the most ambitious systems in the game: **Commander Personalities & AI Doctrine**. This is where autonomy becomes identity — where AI commanders develop distinct styles, preferences, and strategic behaviors that shape the battlefield.

---

## 🧠 Commander Personalities — AI With Identity

Each commander in Obsidian Protocol has a unique personality profile that influences:

- Aggression level  
- Risk tolerance  
- Preferred unit compositions  
- Tactical priorities  
- Reaction speed  
- Adaptation behavior  
- Siege vs. skirmish preference  
- Defensive vs. offensive bias  

These personalities are not cosmetic — they fundamentally change how the AI behaves.

### Examples of personality traits:

- **The Aggressor**  
  Prefers fast assaults, early pressure, and high‑risk maneuvers.

- **The Architect**  
  Builds layered defenses, fortifications, and long‑term economic engines.

- **The Tactician**  
  Uses flanking, suppression, elevation, and coordinated multi‑angle attacks.

- **The Opportunist**  
  Strikes when the player is distracted, overextended, or resource‑starved.

Each personality creates a different battlefield experience.

---

## ⚔️ AI Doctrine — Strategic Frameworks

Doctrine defines *how* a commander fights — the overarching strategy that guides their decisions.

Doctrines include:

- **Siege Doctrine**  
  Focus on breaching, suppression, and structural destruction.

- **Mobility Doctrine**  
  Fast units, hit‑and‑run tactics, flanking, and rapid repositioning.

- **Attrition Doctrine**  
  Slow pressure, resource denial, and grinding down defenses.

- **Combined Arms Doctrine**  
  Balanced forces with coordinated multi‑unit synergy.

- **Autonomy Doctrine**  
  Maximum delegation to squads, minimal direct control.

Doctrines interact with personalities to create unique combinations.

---

## 🔄 Dynamic Doctrine Shifts

Commanders can shift doctrine mid‑battle based on:

- Player behavior  
- Resource flow  
- Unit losses  
- Terrain control  
- Threat evaluation  
- Strategic opportunities  

This makes AI feel alive — not static scripts, but evolving opponents.

---

## 🎮 Why This Matters

Commander personalities and doctrines create:

- Replayability  
- Unpredictability  
- Strategic depth  
- Distinct enemy identities  
- Emergent storytelling  
- Campaign‑scale variety  

Every match feels different because every commander *thinks* differently.
