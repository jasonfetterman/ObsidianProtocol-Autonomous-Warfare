# DEVLOG #10 — The Future of Autonomous RTS

Welcome back to the development journey of **Obsidian Protocol — Autonomous Warfare**.

This devlog looks forward — past the vertical slice, past the reveal, past the campaign — into the long‑term vision for autonomous real‑time strategy. Obsidian Protocol isn’t just a game; it’s a foundation for a new era of RTS design.

Today we explore where this project is heading.

---

## 🚀 The Vision — RTS Without Micromanagement

The core philosophy of Obsidian Protocol is simple:

**Strategy should be about decisions, not babysitting.**

Autonomous squads free players from:

- micro‑movement  
- micro‑targeting  
- micro‑positioning  
- micro‑survival management  

Instead, players focus on:

- intent  
- strategy  
- timing  
- composition  
- territory control  
- doctrine adaptation  

This is the future of RTS — a shift from mechanical execution to strategic expression.

---

## 🧠 Smarter Autonomy — Next‑Gen Battlefield Intelligence

Future updates will expand squad intelligence with:

- deeper contextual evaluation  
- improved threat prediction  
- adaptive formation logic  
- multi‑squad coordination  
- commander‑level strategic influence  
- environmental awareness (weather, hazards, visibility)  

The goal is to make squads feel like trained soldiers, not scripted units.

---

## ⚔️ Evolving AI Commanders

Commander Personalities & Doctrines will grow into:

- multi‑phase battle plans  
- long‑term campaign strategies  
- psychological profiles  
- morale systems  
- rivalry behavior  
- counter‑doctrine adaptation  

Each commander becomes a character — not just an algorithm.

---

The world becomes a strategic opponent.
