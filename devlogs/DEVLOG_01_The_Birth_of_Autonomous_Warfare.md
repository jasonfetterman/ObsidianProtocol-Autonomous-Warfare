# DEVLOG #1 — The Birth of Autonomous Warfare

Welcome to the first official devlog for **Obsidian Protocol — Autonomous Warfare**.

This project began with a simple question:

**Why do RTS units still wait for orders?**

For decades, strategy games have given us bigger armies, prettier explosions, and more complex tech trees — but the battlefield itself never evolved. Units stand still. AI cheats instead of strategizing. Battles feel scripted instead of alive.

I wanted something different.

I wanted a battlefield that **reacts**, **adapts**, and **thinks**.

A battlefield where squads:

- Flank without being told  
- Retreat when overwhelmed  
- Breach walls intelligently  
- Coordinate multi‑angle assaults  
- Use cover, elevation, and suppression  
- Interpret your intent instead of waiting for micro‑orders  

This is the foundation of Obsidian Protocol.

Not a traditional RTS.  
Not a micromanagement simulator.  
But a **living autonomous war engine**.

This devlog marks the beginning of a long journey — one built on autonomy, modularity, and cinematic warfare. If you’re here early, welcome. You’re witnessing the evolution of RTS gameplay.
