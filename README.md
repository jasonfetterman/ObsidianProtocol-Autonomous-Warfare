# 🎖️ THE GAME

**Obsidian Protocol: Autonomous Warfare** is a next-generation multiplayer RTS built around a simple premise:

> **You do not micromanage the battlefield. You command it.**

The player provides **intent, objectives, priorities, doctrine, logistics, and strategic direction**.

Autonomous systems execute those orders across the battlefield.

Units move.

Squads coordinate.

Logistics deliver.

AI adapts.

Recon discovers.

Drones observe.

Command systems distribute information.

The battlefield evolves around the decisions made by the player.

---

# 🧠 CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Obsidian Protocol is designed around the concept of **Command Intent**.

Traditional RTS gameplay often requires the player to constantly issue individual orders:

- Move this unit.
- Attack that target.
- Retreat this squad.
- Gather this resource.
- Build this structure.
- Reposition this vehicle.

Obsidian Protocol moves the focus upward.

Instead of controlling every action, the player defines:

- Objectives
- Priorities
- Rules of engagement
- Formation behavior
- Tactical posture
- Resource priorities
- Logistics priorities
- Recon priorities
- Squad objectives
- Autonomous behavior

The battlefield then becomes a system that responds to those decisions.

---

# ⚔️ WHAT MAKES OBSIDIAN PROTOCOL DIFFERENT

## COMMAND INTENT

The player commands **what should happen**, rather than manually controlling every individual action.

## AUTONOMOUS WARFARE

Units and systems are designed to operate with increasing independence.

## INFORMATION WARFARE

The player does not automatically know everything happening on the battlefield.

Information must be:

- Discovered
- Observed
- Relayed
- Interpreted
- Distributed

## LOGISTICS

Armies require functioning logistics.

Resources move through:

**Extraction → Processing → Logistics → Production → Deployment**

## FOG OF WAR

The battlefield is an intelligence problem.

Enemy locations, movements, strength, and activity are not automatically known.

## MULTI-DOMAIN WARFARE

The project is structured around:

- Ground
- Air
- Sea
- Autonomous systems
- Command systems
- Logistics
- VR/operator systems
